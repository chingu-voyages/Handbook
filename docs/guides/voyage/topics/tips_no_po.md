# My team doesn't have a Product Owner and/or Scrum Master. What should we do❓
![Voyage topic banner](../assets/horizontal-paint-splash-green.jpg)

In a Voyage, Product Owners and Scrum Masters are assigned to teams based on how many participants sign up for these roles. If your team does not have a Product Owner, Scrum Master, or both, don't worry—your team can still successfully complete the Voyage.

The Voyage section of the Handbook guides what needs to be done throughout the Voyage, when each step should take place, and how to complete it. Teams in previous Voyages that were missing one or both of these roles have successfully used one of the following strategies:

1. One team member volunteers to take on the role.
This is generally the preferred option, as it provides clear ownership and consistency throughout the Voyage. Ideally, the person should have some previous experience working with a Product Owner or Scrum Master, or be familiar with the key responsibilities of the role. For example, a Product Owner should be comfortable working with the Product Backlog and writing user stories, while a Scrum Master should be comfortable facilitating team processes and supporting the team in resolving blockers.

2. Rotate the responsibilities between team members each Sprint.
Team members can take turns filling the missing role, allowing everyone to learn and gain experience. If your team chooses this option, make sure there is a clear handoff between Sprints so the next person understands the team's priorities, progress, and any outstanding issues.

3. Team members can agree to share the responsibilities of the missing role.
While having one person take clear ownership of the role is generally preferred, this approach can work if responsibilities are clearly divided and each task has a designated owner. Make sure everyone understands who is responsible for what and communicates regularly to avoid important tasks being overlooked.

Whichever option your team chooses, communication, accountability, and supporting one another are essential. Your team is working toward a shared goal, and everyone's contribution plays a role in your success.

You can find a description of the responsibilities for each of these roles
[here](../topics/voyage_roles.md). In addition, the
[Voyage section](../voyage.md#the-voyage-process) of this Handbook contains a
description of the tasks that need to be performed in each Sprint.

Whichever option you choose, remember that teammates must support one
another -- you will either succeed together or fail together.

Also, you can open a support ticket to get help or advice from an Admin.
